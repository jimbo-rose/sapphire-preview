Our Story

Salt With Purpose

Salt is the one ingredient on every table, at every meal. So we asked a simple question: what if it did a little more?

Made in the mountains

Blended in Park City, Utah.

Sapphire Salt started in Park City with one idea: keep the taste and ritual of salt exactly the same, and make what’s inside the jar more intentional.

We begin with pure Australian ocean sea salt. Then, blend by blend, we add a short list of ingredients chosen on purpose — plant fibers, minerals, amino acids — and nothing that changes how salt tastes.
