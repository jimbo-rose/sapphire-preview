Brooklyn Sapp is the face of Sapphire Salt & Spice. Her dad, Ryan, started the company in Park City with one idea: keep the taste and ritual of salt exactly the same, and make what’s inside the jar more intentional.

Today it’s a father-and-daughter business. Brooklyn is who you’ll meet at markets, on our pages and in your inbox.

What we stand for

Clean down to the container.

Most salt comes in plastic. Most supplements do too. We’re trying hard to keep it out of the jar, and we’ll tell you plainly where we haven’t managed it yet.

  1. Short listsEvery ingredient has a reason, and you can read the whole list in one breath.
  2. Glass and wood firstGlass jars, walnut lids, and paper and cardboard when we ship. No plastic shaker inserts. Lid liner: Ryan to confirm
  3. Honest about plasticOur sticks and packets still need a thin food-safe lining. We say so, and we’re working toward paper-based packets.
  4. Tested, and we show youResults for every batch, starting with heavy metals, posted by lot number. Proposed
  5. Salt does the workNo sweeteners, flavorings or fillers in our salts. The seasonings are real herbs and spices.
