FAQ & Contact

Does it taste different from regular salt?

No — that’s the point. Every blend starts with Australian ocean sea salt, and every added ingredient is chosen so the taste and texture stay familiar.

How do I use it?

Exactly like the salt you use now. Swap it into your shaker or salt cellar 1:1 and season the way you always do.

Which blend should I start with?

The Discovery Collection has every blend in single-serve packets — the easiest way to find your favorite. Pure Sea Salt is the classic; the other four each add a short list of ingredients you can read on our Ingredients page.

What sizes are there?

Every blend comes in a 4 oz shaker jar ($14) and a 16 oz kitchen jar ($28), both glass with wood lids.

Is Sapphire Salt iodized?

No. This salt does not supply iodide, a necessary nutrient.

How long does shipping take?

Orders usually arrive in about 7 business days. Shipping is free over $45; subscriptions, Discovery and Kindness Cards always ship free. If your order hasn’t arrived after two weeks, contact us and we’ll look into it.

What if I don’t love it?

Reach out and we’ll refund you in full. If anything arrives damaged, send a photo and we’ll make it right.

Can I subscribe?

Yes — choose Subscribe & Save on any blend and we’ll ship every two months at 10% off. Skip or cancel anytime.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes. See our wholesale page for the starter kit and line sheet.
